Shift connects the group chat to the schedule

Your team reports it. You see it next to the schedule. You decide.

Shift doesn't replace your chat or your scheduler. It reads what your team already reports, lines it up with what Buffer is about to publish, and makes sure the right person sees it while there's still time.

Built by a social lead who has run posts for a zoo, and who has been on the other end of "why is this still scheduled?"

How it works

1

Anyone on your team types /shift in the group they already use. Guests and staff without GroupMe can use a report link.
