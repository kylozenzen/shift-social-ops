Shift

Your social team shouldn't be the last to know.

The train goes down at 1:10. Your "come ride the train" post goes out at 2:00. Shift puts what your team reports right next to what Buffer is about to publish, so you catch it in time.

Your team reports it

Anyone can type /shift in your GroupMe, or use a report link. No Buffer login, no new app.

You see it against the schedule

Every report lands next to today's and this week's Buffer posts. Posts that mention the same thing get flagged.

You act in one place

Move a post to draft, publish an update now, or leave a note for whoever's on next. Every action is recorded.

Shift runs on Buffer and never publishes on its own. Built by Nobody Creative
