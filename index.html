Shift for Buffer

Your social team shouldn't be the last to know.

When something changes on the ground or in the product, your team hears about it in chat. Shift puts that report right next to what Buffer is about to publish, so you can pull or update the post before it goes out.

Free to start. Sign in with the Buffer account you already use. Shift never publishes on its own.

Why teams use it

Catch it before it publishes

A report that mentions the same thing as a scheduled post gets flagged next to that post. You see the clash while there's still time to fix it.

Act in one place

Move a post to draft, publish an update to the channels you pick, or save an idea to Buffer Ideas. Shift asks you to confirm each public action first.

Put it back when it's fixed

Moved posts return to their original time when you restore them, so the schedule you planned doesn't have to be rebuilt by hand.

Hand off without losing context

Leave notes for whoever is on next, keep a log of what happened, and copy a summary built only from actions that were actually recorded.

What's in it

Reports from chat or a link

Staff type /shift issue, /shift update, /shift idea or /shift request in GroupMe. People without GroupMe use a report link. Add ! to mark something urgent.

Today's schedule on a timeline

Reports sit beside today's Buffer posts on a timeline with a live "now" marker, followed by the rest of the week.

Clash flags that learn your words

Matching is keyword based, not AI. Teach it that "railroad" means "train" and it will flag both. Flags are labeled "might clash," so you make the call.

Setup that tests itself

The first-run card runs a sample report against your real next post, so you see the flag before you rely on it. GroupMe setup includes a Save & test button that says hello in the group.

Built for two kinds of teams

Places with live operations

Zoos, attractions, venues and nonprofits

Your staff know what's happening on the ground before your social team does: a ride down, a closed gate, a lost child, a keeper talk moved. Shift gets that to the person who posts, and it sits next to the post that's about to go out.

  • Front-line staff report from the group they already use
  • Urgent reports are marked and sorted to the top
  • Notes carry across shifts, so the next person knows what's still open

SaaS teams with a social lead

Product companies with one person running social

When there's an incident, an outage or a bug everyone is talking about, engineering and support post it in chat. Shift shows it beside the scheduled posts, so the social lead can hold, reword or publish a status update without digging through five tools.

  • Incidents and outages reported straight from chat
  • Scheduled product posts checked against what's broken right now
  • A handoff summary for whoever covers the next shift or time zone
